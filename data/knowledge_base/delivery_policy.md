# NovaStore - Delivery & Shipping Policy
*Note: This is fictional customer-support policy documentation for the NovaStore demonstration system.*

## Standard Shipping Times
- **Metro Areas:** Orders placed for standard delivery typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days.
- **Regional / Non-Metro Areas:** Deliveries take between 5 to 7 business days.
- **Express Shipping:** Fast-track delivery delivers within 1 to 2 business days for an additional fee of $9.99.

## Order Tracking
- Once your package has dispatched from our fulfillment center, an SMS and email notification containing your unique Tracking Number and live tracking link will be sent to your registered contact details.
- Real-time courier updates become active within 12 hours of dispatch.

## Delayed or Lost Shipments
- If your parcel has not arrived within 7 business days of the projected delivery window, please verify the delivery address in your account.
- If the address is correct and the courier status has not updated in 48 hours, please reach out to customer support or open a support ticket under the **Delivery** category for prompt courier investigation.
- If a parcel is confirmed lost in transit by the carrier, NovaStore offers a 100% free reshipment or a full refund.

## International Delivery
- Currently, NovaStore only ships domestically within our primary operating country. We do not support overseas international shipments at this time.
