# NovaStore - Payment Policy & Billing FAQs
*Note: This is fictional customer-support policy documentation for the NovaStore demonstration system.*

## Accepted Payment Methods
NovaStore accepts all major credit and debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and UPI / direct net banking. Cash on Delivery (COD) is available only in selected zip codes.

## Failed Transactions & Deducted Amounts
- **Issue:** Money was debited from your bank account or card, but no order confirmation was generated.
- **Cause:** This usually occurs due to temporary payment gateway timeout or interrupted network connections between your bank and the payment processor.
- **Resolution:** The payment gateway will automatically reconcile with your bank. The deducted amount is typically reversed back to your source bank account within **3 to 5 business days**.
- **Action Required:** Check your bank statement after 5 business days. If the funds have not been credited back, obtain the Bank Reference Number (UTR / RRN) and create a support ticket under the **Payment** category so our finance team can assist you.

## Double Charges
- In rare circumstances where a customer is charged twice for a single order order ID, NovaStore's payment reconciliation system flags the duplicate payment and releases the duplicate charge within **24 to 48 hours**.

## Invoices & Receipts
- Digital GST/tax invoices are generated automatically upon order placement and can be downloaded from the "Order History" section in your customer account.
