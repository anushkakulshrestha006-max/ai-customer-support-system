# NovaStore - Account & Profile FAQ
*Note: This is fictional customer-support policy documentation for the NovaStore demonstration system.*

## Updating Account Details
- **Email & Phone Number:** You can update your registered email address and primary telephone number under Account > "Profile Settings". A 6-digit one-time password (OTP) verification will be sent to confirm ownership.
- **Shipping Address:** You can add, edit, or remove shipping addresses in "Address Book". Changes made to your address book will apply to all future orders, but will *not* modify the destination of orders that have already dispatched.

## Password Reset & Security
- To reset a forgotten password, click "Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen and enter your registered email address. You will receive a secure password reset link valid for 15 minutes.
- If you notice unauthorized activity on your account, navigate to "Security Settings" and click "Sign out of all sessions", then open a support ticket under the **Account** category immediately.

## Deactivating or Deleting an Account
- To permanently delete your account and associated personal data, submit a request via Account Settings > "Privacy & Data" or open a ticket under the **Account** category.
- Pending orders and active refund cycles must be completed before an account deletion request can be finalized.
