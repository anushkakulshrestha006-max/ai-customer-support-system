# NovaStore - Cancellation Policy
*Note: This is fictional customer-support policy documentation for the NovaStore demonstration system.*

## Cancelling an Order Before Dispatch
- Orders can be cancelled free of charge at any time **before** the parcel has been packed and handed over to the courier (status: "Dispatched" or "Shipped").
- To cancel, navigate to your Account > "My Orders", locate the order, and click "Cancel Order".
- Alternatively, you can request cancellation by contacting support or raising a ticket under the **Cancellation** category with your order ID.

## Cancelling After Dispatch
- Once an order has entered "Shipped" status, it cannot be intercepted or cancelled in the portal.
- You may refuse the delivery at your doorstep when the delivery agent arrives, or accept the package and initiate a return within our 30-day return window.

## Cancellation Refunds
- If you cancel an order that was pre-paid, a full refund of 100% of the invoice amount is automatically triggered.
- Refunds will reflect in the original source payment account within **5 to 7 business days**.
- If loyalty points or promotional voucher codes were applied, points will be credited back to your wallet instantly; voucher codes can be reused on your next purchase.
