# NovaStore - Returns & Refund Policy
*Note: This is fictional customer-support policy documentation for the NovaStore demonstration system.*

## 30-Day Return Window
- Customers are eligible to initiate a return or claim a refund within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date.
- Products must be unused, unwashed, in original packaging, and with all original tags attached.

## Non-Refundable Items
- Perishable goods (food items, flowers), customized/personalized items, downloadable digital software, and opened personal hygiene items cannot be returned or refunded.

## Refund Processing Timeline
- Once our warehouse receives and inspects your returned merchandise, an email confirmation will be sent.
- Approved refunds are credited directly back to the original method of payment within **5 to 7 business days**.
- If paid via Net Banking or Debit/Credit Card, banks may require an additional 2 to 3 days to reflect the credit on your statement.

## Damaged or Defective Items
- If an item arrives broken, defective, or incorrect, report it within **48 hours** of delivery.
- NovaStore will provide a prepaid return shipping label and arrange either an immediate replacement or a 100% refund including original shipping costs.
- Please create a support ticket under the **Refund** category with a brief description of the defect.
